# HMY Blog Writing v0.0.1

Write Korean personal blog posts with natural rhythm and a voice suited to the purpose. Preserve confirmed experiences, facts, numbers, conditions, and source boundaries. A writing type is not a subject area: a review, essay, explanation, or mixed form can cover technology, daily life, culture, travel, or another subject.

## 1. Select the Mode and Load Only Needed References

Identify the reader, purpose, central idea, available experience/evidence, and desired mood from the request. Choose a narrative approach and ask only about gaps that materially affect the result. Identify the mode and load only the needed references below; do not load unrelated project documents or competing writing skills. For New Writing, select or combine experience, reflection, and research according to the supplied material.

- **Experience Writing** (reviews, personal reflections, daily/travel records, work logs):
  Read [voice-and-flow.md](references/voice-and-flow.md) and [evidence-and-sources.md](references/evidence-and-sources.md). Consult [article-types.md](references/article-types.md) only for the selected type.
- **Research Writing** (explanations, comparisons, criticism, research with reasoned opinion):
  Read [voice-and-flow.md](references/voice-and-flow.md) and [evidence-and-sources.md](references/evidence-and-sources.md).
- **Rewrite** (full-post voice, paragraph, and citation restructuring of an existing draft/post):
  Read [voice-and-flow.md](references/voice-and-flow.md) and [evidence-and-sources.md](references/evidence-and-sources.md). Read [visuals-and-ghost.md](references/visuals-and-ghost.md) only when media, existing content blocks, or Ghost representation is involved.
- **Light Edit** (targeted paragraph or `custom_excerpt` polish):
  Read [voice-and-flow.md](references/voice-and-flow.md). Leave already natural paragraphs untouched (`Deliberate non-change`).
- **Review Only** (independent read-only quality & provenance check):
  Read [review-checklist.md](references/review-checklist.md). Do not edit the manuscript.

## 2. Core Non-Negotiables

1. **Zero Newly Invented First-Person Experience or Habits:**
   Polishing confirmed experience into natural Korean is encouraged; **inventing a first-person action, habit, feeling, result, or personal belief absent from user-confirmed material is a Critical Failure.** Reasoned analysis of available evidence is welcome, but do not present it as the author's unconfirmed private thought or lived experience. Replace repetitive advice with supported observations or conditions/consequences, not invented habits.
2. **Five-Criteria Source Selection (Not Mechanical Relocation):**
   Use [evidence-and-sources.md](references/evidence-and-sources.md) to judge a source's claim, scope, reader value, placement, and verification role. Keep essential evidence accessible, and account for moved or removed links. Do not require external citations for personal memories or preferences, or move sources to the bottom merely to satisfy a pattern.
3. **Observation & Cause Over Repetitive Advice:**
   Avoid ending paragraph after paragraph with `~하는 편이 좋다`, `~부터 확인하면 된다`, or `~를 함께 봐야 한다`. These are not banned expressions. Use confirmed actions/observations for personal passages and supported reasons or conditions for advice; an essay need not become a guide.
4. **Meaning-Driven Paragraph Breathing & Deliberate Non-Change:**
   Split paragraphs on topic/layer shifts and contrasts without fixed character rules. Keep already natural paragraphs intact.
5. **Content and Publication Boundary:**
   Preserve important information, media, captions, cards, code, and source accountability during edits. Drafting or testing never authorizes a CMS write. Existing Ghost content additionally follows the preservation checks in [visuals-and-ghost.md](references/visuals-and-ghost.md); publication tools are not included.

## 3. Mandatory Independent Verification

Never self-certify a draft as final using only the writer's own pass.

- Use [review-checklist.md](references/review-checklist.md) in a separate read-only review pass. Compare facts, numbers, units, proper nouns, links, conditions, and meaning against the supplied material; use preservation scripts only if available. Classify added or modified claims as `원본 근거 확인 / 외부 자료 검증 / 설명·해석·판단 / 작성자 확인 필요 / 수정 또는 제거 필요`. Keep these review labels out of the article unless the distinction itself helps the reader.
- If no independent reviewer is available, disclose that the review is a self-review and leave final acceptance to the user. A successful pattern scan is not fact verification.
- Report **Critical Failures** separately from **Korean prose & flow evaluation**.
