# Evidence Discipline and Five-Criteria Source Selection

## 1. Five-Way Evidence Distinction

Keep five categories distinct in both Experience Writing and Research Writing:

1. **Actual User Experience (`실제 사용자 경험`):** Facts, models, counts, settings, and episodes confirmed by the user. Never invent unconfirmed habits, routines, or sensations.
2. **Verified External Fact (`확인된 외부 사실`):** Information checked against appropriate sources: primary documents, original works, research, public data, or official specifications as the claim requires. Preserve model, version, date, place, population, and other applicable scope.
3. **Author's Evaluation & Judgment (`작성자의 평가와 판단`):** Interpretation grounded in confirmed experience, supplied material, or verified information.
4. **Hypothesis (`검증되지 않은 가설`):** A plausible explanation not established by evidence. Mark the uncertainty without presenting it as a confirmed cause or result.
5. **Unknown Information (`미확인 정보`):** Details not recorded at the time. Leave unknown or omit.

Personal recollections and preferences do not need a manufacturer manual or an external citation. Check externally verifiable claims when they matter to the reader. Technical specifications and safety conditions still require appropriately scoped evidence; a rule for one model is not a rule for all products.

## 2. Five-Criteria Source Selection

Moving official links from the body to the bottom is not a goal in itself. Evaluate each source against five questions:

1. **Does it back a core claim of this post?** (`해당 글의 핵심 주장을 뒷받침하는가?`)
2. **What limits its scope?** (`특정 모델·버전·시점·지역·대상에만 적용되는가?`)
3. **Does a reader actually gain value from consulting it?** (`독자가 실제로 참고할 가치가 있는가?`)
4. **Is it essential to link right at that sentence in the body?** (`본문에서 바로 연결해야 하는 필수 근거인가?`)
5. **Was it merely an auxiliary check during internal research?** (`단순히 내부 조사 과정에서 확인한 보조 자료인가?`)

### Placement Decision by Criteria
- **Keep Inline:** Sources needed to verify a central claim, quotation, exact condition, or safety limit at that point; direct service/download links when the reader needs to act.
- **Keep in Bottom `참고자료` (or Existing Bookmark Cards):** Useful background or supporting material that does not need to interrupt the passage. Do not introduce every source with a formal document-summary sentence.
- **Exclude from Public Prose (Keep in Internal Ledger & `link_changes`):** Sources that apply only to an unrelated model (for example, a different device's torque specification cited in a general tool explanation) or redundant links that add no reader value. Never let source exclusion weaken the technical accuracy or safety boundary in the text.
